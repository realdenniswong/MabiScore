Settings

Keyboard input

Note layout

Z / X and the octave buttons work in both layouts. Shift+= raises the octave; Shift+− lowers it. R inserts a rest. Notes start at the playhead and advance by the new-note length.

Editing

Select moves existing notes; Draw adds notes; Erase deletes notes and leaves the time in place. Delete / Backspace removes the selection. Arrow keys move it; Shift+↑ / ↓ moves one octave.

Score display

The score wraps into grand-staff systems in 4/4, with rests, beams, accidentals and ties. The same notes are used by the roll, score and export.

Drafts & recovery

Edits are saved automatically in this browser. Download a backup to keep a separate copy or move your score to another device.

Active track & sound

Choose the instrument beside the track name. Track volume applies to every note in that track.

Delete time range

Remove a period from all tracks and move everything after it earlier to close the gap. Notes crossing the edges are shortened. Undo restores the cut.

Bar numbers start at 1. For example, from bar 2 to bar 4 removes bars 2 and 3. Decimals select part of a bar.

Quick guide

Make your first score

1

Add & arrangeChoose Draw to add notes, Select to move them, or Erase to remove them. Select a note to change pitch, starting beat and length. Delete keeps the time in place; Undo restores the note.

2

Type & refineTurn on Keys (⌘/Ctrl+K). Settings offers Piano keys or MML letters (C–B, then = for sharp or − for flat). Keep Z/X or use Shift+= / Shift+− for octave. R adds a rest; on-screen keys work on touch.

3

Tracks & exportUse MIDI → 3 MML to reduce a MIDI file to three non-overlapping parts and open the MML export. Extra simultaneous notes may be omitted. Space plays the score, and every edit supports Undo and Redo.
